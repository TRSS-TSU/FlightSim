Key systems and how they fit together

Scenario-driven training flow

ScenarioDefinition is a ScriptableObject schema with scenario title/route/description, map anchor, tile preload settings, waypoint database, prefilled route idents, approach fixes, and performance data. The observed asset Assets/ScriptableObjects/ScenarioDefinition01.asset is titled Flight Scenario 01, uses route T3002, centers around latitude 30.3 / longitude -87.3 at zoom 14, and describes a departure from KNPA to Visual Route 1020 with landing at KVPS.

ScenarioRuntime is a static holder for the current scenario. Its Set method validates the scenario and raises OnChanged, allowing the flight plan, FMS, and navigation systems to react to scenario changes.

Flight plan and map/tile systems

FlightPlan listens to ScenarioRuntime.OnChanged, can build a waypoint route from a selected scenario, and supports training-world scaling through trainingWorldScale. It also handles snapping/stabilizing the aircraft at scenario start. The project includes Assets/Prefabs/GroundTile_Z16.prefab, Assets/Prefabs/ND_Tile.prefab, Assets/Prefabs/WP_Marker.prefab, and 7,738 PNG map tiles in Assets/StreamingAssets/tiles_nd_fms_v2.

FMS/CDU architecture

FmsModel is intentionally pure C# and acts as the central data store for FMS state: active route, origin/destination, POS INIT data, frequencies, status database identifiers, performance values, and live telemetry. FmsPageRouter is the MonoBehaviour scene controller that owns an FmsModel, registers page views, shows pages, and pumps live telemetry from FlightDataBus at 10 Hz. Page scripts such as ActFplnView, ActLegsView, DepArrView, DirView, PerfInitView, PosInitView, ProgView, StatusView, and TuneView render CDU pages and handle student interactions.

Aircraft motion, autopilot, and telemetry

PlaneController requires a Rigidbody and implements training-scaled movement, speed hold, altitude capture/hold, VNAV-lite parameters, turn dynamics, visual pitch, and parked pose behavior. NavAutopilot follows FlightPlan waypoints, exposes active distance/bearing/cross-track error, supports NAV engagement, waypoint capture, look-ahead LNAV, and turn anticipation. FlightDataBus is the central telemetry hub for instruments, exposing unit-suffixed values such as iasKt, gsKt, altFtMsl, hdgDeg, trkDeg, vsiFpm, bankDeg, pitchDeg, brgDeg, and distM.

Navigation display and instruments

NdPresenter positions an ND camera above the aircraft and builds the route line from FlightPlan.waypoints. Additional ND helpers include NDAircraftIconDriver, NDRangeState, and WebMercator. PFD/instrument scripts under Assets/Scripts/Instruments drive displayed attitude, heading rose/bug, airspeed tape, altitude tape/readout, lower data, pitch needle, selected altitude, and vertical speed scale.

Takeoff procedure

TakeoffProcedureController coordinates staged takeoff targets. It references SimTargets, NavAutopilot, an aircraft transform, and FlightPlan; it uses runway/departure headings, speed/altitude targets, timing, and waypoint gates such as KNPA_RW25R_THRESH and KNPA_DEP_1DME.

Developer orientation guide

  1. Start with scenes. Open Assets/Scenes/Menu.unity to understand scenario selection. Open Assets/Scenes/Master_FMS.unity to inspect the full simulator scene and wired references.
  2. Trace scenario flow. Follow ScenarioSelection → ScenarioRuntime.Set → FlightPlan.LoadScenario / FmsPageRouter.OnScenarioChanged / NavAutopilot.OnScenarioChanged.
  3. Trace flight truth. Inspect PlaneController, SimTargets, and NavAutopilot. These drive the actual movement targets and route following.
  4. Trace display truth. Inspect FlightDataBus. Instrument and FMS page scripts should read from this hub rather than duplicating unit conversions.
  5. Work on the CDU by page. FmsPageRouter owns navigation and state; concrete page scripts under Assets/Scripts/FMS/Pages should render and mutate the model through the router.
  6. Be careful with map scale. There are explicit training-world and movement scale controls; do not assume real-world meters equal game-world spacing everywhere.
  7. Validate scenario assets. ScenarioDefinitionValidator is invoked by ScenarioRuntime.Set. If adding routes or waypoints, keep identifiers consistent with FMS prefill route idents, takeoff gates, and approach sets.

The current editor scene is the menu, not the full simulator scene. Some runtime wiring for aircraft/FMS/ND systems should be verified by opening Master_FMS.unity before making major scene-reference changes.
