Phase 1

Scenario setup and initialization

Load the scenario, establish the FMS position, and review the scenario-provided performance data.

1

Load Flight Scenario 01

  1. From the Menu, select Flight Scenario 01.
  2. Review the scenario confirmation panel.
  3. Select Yes to enter Master_FMS.

The aircraft should load parked at KNPA. Selecting No returns to the prior menu panel without loading the scenario.

2

Complete POS INIT

  1. Open POS INIT.
  2. On page 1/2, select right LSK 4 beside SET POS TO GNSS.
  3. Wait for the position load to complete; the line changes to COMPLETED.
  4. Select right LSK 6 beside FPLN> when ready to continue.

Opening POS INIT records the POS INIT check. NEXT and PREV switch between its two pages.

3

Review PERF INIT

  1. Open PERF INIT and stay on page 1/2.
  2. Review ZFW 11,600 lb, fuel 4,300 lb, and the computed gross weight.
  3. To change ZFW or fuel, enter a non-negative value in the scratchpad and select left LSK 1 or 2.
  4. If a value was changed, press EXEC and confirm the PERF ACCEPTED message.

Opening PERF INIT records both the fuel and weight/ZFW checks. V-speeds on page 2 are marked NOT IMPL.

Phase 2

Program, execute, and verify the route

The FMS preserves a deliberate ACT/MOD workflow. EXEC activates the route; it does not launch the aircraft.

  1. 1

    Open ACT FPLN

    Press FPLN. The displayed scenario route name is T3002.

  2. 2

    Select the route

    With an empty scratchpad, select left LSK 2 once to copy the scenario route name. Select left LSK 2 again to enter MOD FPLN.

  3. 3

    Arm EXEC

    Select left LSK 6 beside <YES. The MOD route is now armed.

  4. 4

    Activate

    Press the physical EXEC key. Wait for ROUTE LOADED and the route-review popup.

  5. 5

    Review ACT LEGS

    Verify the waypoint order, then choose Checks Complete. Choosing Cancel leaves takeoff locked and returns you to ACT LEGS.

Required before takeoff review

The five checks

  1. 01POS INIT page viewed
  2. 02PERF INIT fuel viewed
  3. 03PERF INIT weight/ZFW viewed
  4. 04Route successfully executed
  5. 05Active route review confirmed

Important: POS/PERF checks are measured, but route review is the hard takeoff gate. Missing checks are counted when takeoff starts.

Optional RNAV 25L setup: Open DEP ARR, select right LSK 4 beside LOAD 25L>, then press EXEC to commit the staged MOD. Any pre-takeoff route change requires another active-route review.

Phases 3–5

Takeoff, enroute, hold, and land

Once the route review is confirmed, the Start control appears and the simulator can fly the active guidance.

TakeoffAutomated departure
  1. Select Start. The active route and completed route review are required.
  2. The aircraft rolls on runway heading, lifts off, and begins the initial climb.
  3. At 250 ft, NAV engages and hands the aircraft to the active FMS route.
  4. Monitor the PFD and ND as guidance continues toward TEEZY.
EnrouteFollow the active legs

TEEZY → TRADR → BFM → VR1020_A → B → C → D → E → CEW → PENSI

Watch ACT LEGS and the ND for sequencing. Each valid waypoint completion is recorded. PENSI is the transition trigger; it is not part of the holding loop.

PENSIEnter the hold

At PENSI, the runtime route changes to the four-fix loop:

CUPER → POOVE → APUCE → ALCOME ↻

The HOLD DECISION appears at CUPER. A completed CUPER-to-ALCOME circuit increments the hold-circuit result.

Continue Holding

Closes the decision and leaves the loop unchanged. The decision returns at the next CUPER.

Begin Landing

Arms the exit, appends the landing points once, and finishes the current hold circuit.

RNAV 25LLanding sequence
  1. After Begin Landing, continue through the current loop to ALCOME.
  2. At ALCOME, guidance advances to KNPA_RW25L_FINAL instead of returning to CUPER.
  3. The final target is 1,200 ft / 200 kt.
  4. Guidance then advances to KNPA_RW25L_TOUCHDOWN at 100 kt.
  5. At touchdown, the aircraft enters Landing and proceeds to KNPA_FINAL_STOP.

The simulator marks touchdown and final stop, disengages NAV, stops the aircraft on the ground, and displays the completion choices.

Phase 6

Completion and Scenario Results

At the final stop, choose whether to repeat the scenario or preserve the completed record and review it.

↻

Takeoff Again

Reloads the same scenario, clears the flight record, returns to parked preflight, and requires route review again.

→

End

Preserves the finalized record and opens the ScenarioResults scene.

Finalized by the flight session

What the simulator tracks

The results screen displays the record; it does not recalculate it.

  • Scenario name and status
  • Elapsed time
  • Waypoints completed
  • Route modifications
  • Skipped required checks
  • Hold circuits completed
  • Touchdown reached
  • Final stop reached

From Scenario Results: choose Back to Menu to clear the record and return to the Menu, or End to quit the application.
